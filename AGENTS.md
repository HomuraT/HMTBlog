# Daily paper digest editorial rules

These rules apply only to `src/content/posts/study/paper/daily/`.

- Publish reader-facing research writing: the problem, method, evidence, experimental conditions, comparisons, limitations, interpretation, and useful reading links.
- Keep report-generation and publication operations out of article text, descriptions, captions, and headings. Do not include backfill/handoff narratives, candidate-count or merge bookkeeping, source-fetch errors/HTTP diagnostics, internal verification timestamps/checklists, build/CI/deployment status, publication commit IDs, local paths, attachment inventories, mail identifiers, or delivery status. Keep operational records in private task output outside the public repository.
- Do not confuse research about logs, debugging, benchmarks, validation, or system deployment with operational narration about producing this blog. Preserve substantive research discussion.
- Preserve paper titles, public source links, cited versions and publication dates, experimental conditions, uncertainty, and negative results. Keep a concise author-reported/not-independently-reproduced caveat where relevant; do not narrate the editorial verification process or claim complete coverage.
- Preserve figures and attribution: authors, paper/version, figure/table number, source page, license, and crop/alteration information. Do not remove copyright or license disclosures for reproduced material. When a figure is not reproduced, a direct source-page link is sufficient; do not publish internal rights-review or download troubleshooting notes.
- Before publishing, review the actual rendered article for internal process text, raw Markdown markers, broken links and missing images. Keep publication status separate from email status and avoid duplicate posts or messages.

Do not change unrelated articles or task credentials, network/security settings, or mail behavior as part of editorial cleanup.
