# Daily paper article editing

Scope: `src/content/posts/study/paper/daily/` only.

- Write research content for readers. Keep report generation, discovery/selection counts, backfill/handoff notes, verification checklists, access diagnostics, and mail/build/publication status in private task outputs, outside articles and excerpts.
- Preserve substantive paper introductions, methods, experimental findings and conditions, scientific limitations, figures, public sources, versions and attribution. Do not delete these while removing internal process commentary.
- Review changes paragraph by paragraph. For a paragraph mixing research and process narration, remove only the specific process prefix/suffix. Never delete an entire section because its heading mentions verification or provenance. Compare protected rendered prose, tables, links and every figure before publication.
- Keep figure captions concise: author/paper, figure or table number, source page/link, license, and a short alteration note such as “裁剪自原文”. Avoid repeated endorsement and site-license boilerplate.

These rules do not change mail behavior, scheduling, credentials or network/security settings.
